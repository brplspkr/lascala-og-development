LA SCALA OG 1977 - R2 REVIEW RESPONSE

R2 is a reference reconstruction, not a verified manufacturing release.

1. FOUR REAR WING HALVES
Changed the air-contact face slope from 31.2392149 degrees to exactly 30 degrees.
Throat-edge starts remain Z=136.525 / 466.725 mm. Motorboard side edges remain X=+/-193.675 mm.
At those side edges, lower/upper face endpoints are Z=24.7066866 / 578.5433134 mm. Each is 5.6566866 mm from its original floor/shelf corner.
The stock faces are trimmed against the existing horizontal floor/shelf, so the far ends have a finite edge rather than an invented extension or moved throat. The air-face endpoint does not land at the corner.
The source's 30-degree miter note is honored in the face slope for this revision, but the low-resolution source does not independently settle the original wing endpoint or all saw-bevel details. These remain to verify before manufacture.

2. MIDRANGE CUTOUT
Drawing image checked directly: width is 16-3/16 inches, not 16-3/8.
Retained 411.1625 mm width x 142.875 mm height, centered at Z=715.16875 mm.
The 16-3/8 dimension elsewhere on the sheet labels doghouse roof panel #5, not the midrange cutout.
The upper motorboard is geometrically identical to R1.

3. SEPARATE SPEAKER BOTTOM #11
Removed the provisional six-sided flush plug.
Added a separate rectangular 23 x 23-1/2 x 3/4 inch panel beneath the existing woofer-door frame, at Z=-19.05 to 0 mm.
User explicitly approved 36-inch complete height with this underlaid cover.
Existing main cabinet remains 23-3/4 W x 24-1/2 D x 35-1/4 H inches.
Complete R2 including cover is 603.25 W x 622.3 D x 914.4 H mm (36 inches H).
The cover is centered under the complete cabinet footprint. Its exact fore/aft offset, screw pattern, gasket profile and factory thickness are not dimensioned on this scan. Centering is an explicit R2 assumption; thickness and increased height are user-approved choices, not a claim about factory construction.
The source calls for a screwed, unglued cover and weatherstrip roughly 1 inch out from the opening. Fasteners and gasket are not modeled as verified details.

UNCHANGED / CHECKED
13 protected solid parts are geometrically identical to R1 by symmetric-difference volume, including both sides, top, back, shelf, woofer-door frame, woofer motorboard, both straight doghouse sides, both angled nose panels, splitter and upper motorboard.
No positive-volume cabinet panel intersections were found.
Cabinet-only STEP reimported as 18 valid solids; full assembly reimported as 21 valid solids.
Native file reopened and the four wing sketch face slopes were independently measured at 30 degrees.

CROSSOVER SCOPE - USER CLARIFICATION
This project will use a passive crossover: either an original La Scala network or a compatible upgraded passive network.
The exact crossover design is not selected yet and must match the final woofer, midrange driver/horn and tweeter.
DSP and per-band DSP delay settings are outside this project's intended configuration. The geometric delay estimates in the external review are not design targets or pending setup work.
No cabinet geometry was changed for this clarification.
